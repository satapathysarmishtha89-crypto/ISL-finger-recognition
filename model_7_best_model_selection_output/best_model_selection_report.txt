=========================================================================================================
DETAILED ARCHITECTURAL COMPARISON & EMPIRICAL FINDINGS
=========================================================================================================
1. PLANNED CANDIDATE CONFIRMATION: Hybrid Late Fusion (Weighted Average, w=0.69)
   - Peak Standard Accuracy:   99.96% (Macro-F1: 0.9996) -> HIGHEST ACROSS ALL ARCHITECTURES.
   - Error Count:              Only 2 misclassifications out of 5,188 test images (0.04% error rate).
   - Joint Disambiguation:     Successfully resolves ambiguous signs where CNN visual features alone
                               hesitated (e.g., distinguishing Sign 'Y' from Sign '6').
   - Multi-Modal Synergy:      Directly couples image pixels with 42-D anatomical finger coordinates,
                               enabling dual Grad-CAM heatmaps AND Gemini sign tutoring.
   - Signer-Independent Acc:   95.62% (+27.10% higher than pure Landmark DNN).

2. OUT-OF-DISTRIBUTION ROBUSTNESS LEADER: CNN (EfficientNet-B0)
   - Signer-Independent Acc:   99.42% (Macro-F1: 0.9946) -> Generalization drop of only +0.52%.
   - Background-Independent Acc: 98.64% (Macro-F1: 0.9873) -> Generalization drop of only +1.30%.
   - Strength:                 Exceptional compound scaling filters out illumination and background variations.
   - Limitation:               20.17 MB size and lacks explicit landmark coordinates for anatomical tutoring.

3. REAL-TIME MOBILE & EDGE LEADER: CNN (MobileNetV2)
   - Footprint:                3.50M parameters (13.37 MB), 34% smaller than EfficientNet-B0.
   - CPU Latency:              0.0040 ms/image (real-time 60+ FPS on mobile devices).
   - Accuracy:                 99.94% standard, 97.78% signer-independent, 96.50% background-independent.
   - Role:                     Serves as the high-efficiency CNN backbone in the Hybrid Late Fusion model.

4. GEOMETRIC LANDMARK SENSITIVITY: Landmark DNN (42-D)
   - Peak Standard Accuracy:   97.59% with ultra-lightweight 0.06 MB footprint (16K parameters).
   - Generalization Drop:      Drops to 68.52% on unseen signers due to varying hand and bone proportions.
   - Value:                    Provides vital joint geometry evidence that stabilizes the hybrid model.
